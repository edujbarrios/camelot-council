# Startup Strategy Demo

## Command

`/roundtable --triad startup Should we sell to startups first or enterprise teams?`

## Selected Agents

Arthur, Morgana, Lancelot

## Compact Round Output

- Arthur: Choose the segment that best matches the product promise and sales capacity.
- Morgana: Enterprise interest can distort the roadmap before product-market fit is real.
- Lancelot: Startups allow faster learning, shorter cycles, and cheaper onboarding tests.

## Final Verdict

Start with startups for learning speed, but document enterprise requests as signal. Reassess after ten qualified pilots and one repeatable activation path.
